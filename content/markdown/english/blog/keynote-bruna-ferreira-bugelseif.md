## Exciting news!

We already have our first keynote confirmed, and it’s none other than the amazing and talented **Bruna Ferreira**.  
**PS**: You might know her better by her handle **bug_elseif**, right?

{{< image src="images/posts/bugelseif/2024.jpg" alt="Bruna at the 2024 Edition" >}}

## Who is Bruna Ferreira?

Bruna is a software developer and DevX at BotCity. We already know her for her work as a content creator and as an active voice in the Python community. For all these reasons, she’s the perfect choice for our keynote.

With a background as a Technician in Internet Informatics and a degree in Computer Science, she brings solid technical expertise and a sharp eye for community needs. Bruna runs a YouTube channel (**@bug_elseif**) and collaborates with communities such as Feministech and CodeShow, always focusing on Python and automation.

## What she teaches (and why it matters)

If you follow **Bruna**, you’ve probably seen how she turns complex topics into simple, actionable steps.  

{{< iframe src="https://player.twitch.tv/?video=2450669601&parent=sul.python.org.br" title="Git init - An introduction to basic Git commands" >}}

### A few examples:

-   In one of her livestreams, she showed how to get started with consuming APIs in Python, using `requests` for HTTP calls.
-   In another, she talked about careers in RPA, sharing advice and opportunities directly from the BotCity team.
-   In her mini-course on Docker for smart automation, she clearly explained how to use containers to scale automation projects.
-   In a hands-on article, she presented Twilio + WhatsApp with Python, demonstrating how to integrate APIs into everyday development.

Her body of work shows a strong focus on practical content, with a teaching style that welcomes both beginners and career changers.
