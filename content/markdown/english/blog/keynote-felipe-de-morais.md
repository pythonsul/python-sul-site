We’re thrilled to wrap up our keynote announcements for **Python Sul 2025** with an incredible guest: **Felipe de Morais**! 

{{< image src="images/posts/felipedemorais/felipe-de-morais-cover.jpg" alt="Felipe de Morais" >}}

---

Recognized for his inspiring journey and remarkable contributions to the Python community, Felipe is a reference when it comes to web frameworks, community building, and inclusion. And it’s no surprise, he has earned several awards for his impact, including:

- Dornelles Trémea Award (APyB)  
- Community Service Award (PSF)  
- PSF Fellow 2025/Q1  

Co-founder of **AfroPython**, Felipe is a passionate advocate for diversity and inclusion in technology, creating opportunities for underrepresented groups. He is also a teacher, speaker, and known affectionately in the community as **FeliPython**, a nickname that reflects his long-standing dedication to the Python ecosystem.

## Who is Felipe de Morais?

{{< image src="images/posts/felipedemorais/felipe-de-morais-afropython-banner.jpg" alt="Felipe de Morais" className="image-banner" >}}

Born in Rio de Janeiro and now living in **Porto Alegre** for over a decade, Felipe proudly calls the city home.  
He is the dad of **Paçoca 🐶**, a **purple belt in Jiu-Jitsu 🥋**, and a true problem-solver, always driven by the desire to make a difference.

Early on, Felipe even considered studying Law to fight against injustice. But it was in technology that he found his path. He started as an intern at major companies, discovered **Python and Django**, and never looked back. From there, he immersed himself in open source and the local community.

Seeing that women were still underrepresented in tech, he got involved with initiatives like **Django Girls**. But he felt there was still more to be done, and that is when the idea that changed everything came along: **founding AfroPython**, a project that continues to empower Black people through technology to this day.

## Talks and Workshops

If there is one thing Felipe loves, it is sharing knowledge. He has traveled across Brazil and beyond to deliver talks and workshops that inspire people everywhere. He has been part of events like **DjangoCon**, **PyCon US**, **Python Brasil**, and many regional Python conferences, always representing his community with pride.
