## Meet Avidity

We’re excited to announce **Avidity** as a sponsor of Python Sul 2025!  

Founded in 2012, Avidity is a Swedish-Brazilian-German software company helping businesses across Europe build robust, innovative solutions.  

Their team is diverse and multidisciplinary, covering development, DevOps, cloud operations, integration, and more. They work with software companies, digital agencies, financial institutions, and educational platforms.  

---

## Why it matters

Avidity embodies **technology, diversity, and innovation**, values that align perfectly with Python Sul.  

By supporting our event, they reinforce the importance of community-driven Free Software and Open Source initiatives.  

We’re thrilled about this partnership and can’t wait for attendees to connect with Avidity during Python Sul 2025.
